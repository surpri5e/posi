Cat Care Tips & Advice

Helpful tips and advice to keep your fur babies happy and healthy.

  • Regular grooming reduces shedding and hairballs.
  • Provide fresh water 24/7 to keep your cat hydrated.
  • Train your cat to use a scratching post
  • Use interactive toys to keep your cat mentally stimulated.
  • Feed a balanced diet specific to your cat’s age and health.
  • Consider spaying or neutering your cat
  • Make sure your cat always has a place to potty
  • Keep the litter box clean
  • Choose a cat-friendly vet
  • Schedule regular vet checkups to catch health issues early.
  • Keep your cat indoors or supervise outdoor time for safety.
  • Use a cat carrier in the car
